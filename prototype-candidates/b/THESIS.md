# Candidate B · Paper Trail

Thesis: Databricks' true brand canvas is oat, not navy. The one light-mode candidate turns daylight into the premium position: broadsheet masthead, Swiss column grid, documents as physical paper with soft shadows, and Databricks red used only the way an editor uses a pen (the circle around $3,310, the underline under "ten minutes ago", the CAUGHT stamp).

Why it wins in a boardroom: projectors wash out dark UIs at 10 AM; this reads like a signed audit report, not a screensaver. Red does exactly one job per screen, so the eye lands on the money and the catch first, every time. GA/Beta stamps, wall-clock receipts, and the "nothing simulated" folio line make honesty part of the aesthetic, which is the actual sales argument.

Sacrificed: dark-mode drama, glow effects, and dashboard density. No neon pipelines; motion is reserved for marching belt dashes and pulse dots, because a page this quiet makes the one circled number louder.
