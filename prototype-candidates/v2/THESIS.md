1. Routing is drawn as a switchyard: one heavy stream hits the labeler and fans into four curved paths, so the geometry itself teaches the routing logic before any copy is read.

6. Calm is achieved by subtraction: two neutral lanes, one gradient, monochrome status badges, no boxes around destinations, and the two largest whitespace pockets left deliberately empty.
7. Badges stay honest to platform_facts (GA vs Public Preview), the counter and money board replay run_metrics, and the footer says so in plain words.
