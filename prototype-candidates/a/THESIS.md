# SIGNAL TOWER

Thesis: DocFlow as an international departure hall. Documents are flights, pipeline stages are check-in and gates, lanes are gate signs (E1 extract, A2 audit, S3 secure, X0 archive), statuses land in split-flap letters. Flow is the departures board; chat is the arrivals board where answers land with wall-clock receipts.

Why it wins in a boardroom: a projector demo is theater, and nothing reads from the back row like an arrivals board. One monumental surface, zero chrome, extreme typographic discipline; every glance lands on a fact (47 docs, parsing 6 of 47, $3,310 caught vs $0.61 cost). Red fires only at signal moments: a held claim, the caught money, TEN MINUTES AGO, the one primary action. The metaphor also carries the platform story honestly: bricks and previews are gates you open, GA plumbing is the conveyor that always runs.

Sacrificed: soft-UI comfort (cards, gradients-as-decoration, illustration), per-document drill-down surfaces, and any pretense of mobile. This is a wall display built for 12 feet and 12 seconds, not a lap screen.
