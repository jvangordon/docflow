# V1 · Swimlane Highway

Lane = row = one Agent Bricks capability, so the whole routing story is readable in four horizontal strokes from the back of a boardroom.
The canvas is a typeset schedule, not a dashboard: open hairline tracks, one glowing panel, and whitespace doing the separating that boxes usually do.
Lane 3 (IE + KA together) gets the only lava treatment and triple height because composition is the pitch; its hero card IS the explainability centerpiece.
The routing decision record renders as a plain six-field grid plus one "why" sentence, so the audience sees the classifier argue its own routing out loud.
Chips carry exactly five facts (id, type, confidence, sensitivity, capability marks) and nothing else; volume lives in tallies, not clutter.
Color is rationed to meaning: lava = the live show (primary action, caught dollars, lane 3), blue = secure, everything else oat on navy.
Every status badge is honest per platform_facts (IE Public Preview on ai_extract GA, KA GA, ai_mask Public Preview, Genie Agents GA) and shapes bind to /api/state.
The quiet junk tail closes the argument: sometimes the right answer is no AI at all, and the canvas only ever replays recorded run_metrics.
