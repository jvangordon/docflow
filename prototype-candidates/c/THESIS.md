# Candidate C · Document Theater

**Thesis:** Follow one document, not the fleet. WC-2214 stands center stage under a lava spotlight at its labeling moment; played scenes (inbox, parse) recede upstage in scale and opacity, the four lanes glow ahead as lit doors, and the other 41 docs are a constellation in the wings. Every stage transition is a scene change; the run-of-show rail is the script.

**Why it wins in a boardroom:** executives follow protagonists, not topologies. One hero doc with real field values, a visible re-score (0.81 fallback to 0.97 brick), and a single loud CTA gives the room exactly one thing to watch per beat, while the money board ($3,310 caught / $0.61 cost) and wall-clock receipts (9:41 cold ask, 9:44 tables live, 9:51 answered) make the Act 3 prestige land as proof, not vibes. Supervisor routing is staged as the decision itself, drawn large, with GA/Beta badges kept honest.

**What I sacrificed:** the always-on fleet overview. You cannot inspect 47 docs at once here; scale is felt ambiently (ticker, wings, lane counters) rather than surveyed, and per-doc drill-down for the non-hero docs waits offstage.
