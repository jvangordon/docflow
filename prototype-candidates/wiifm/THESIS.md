# WIIFM: Monday Morning
Thesis: the demo proves the tech, this page proves the point. It answers one question, "what does Acme do with this on Monday morning," in three quiet moves: the tables you now own, the work already routed to your teams, and the measured math of one run.
It sells without hype because every line is a noun the buyer already owns (claims team, AP, quality, Unity Catalog) attached to a number the pipeline actually produced (19 rows, 2 invoices, $3,310, $0.61).
Honesty is styled as confidence: the only red pixel on the page is the money caught, the 4-hour comparison prints its assumption and arithmetic beside it, and the synthetic-data disclosure sits in the closing line of sight.
The calm is the argument: nothing animates, no zone holds more than four elements, and the whitespace says "boring infrastructure you can run tomorrow" louder than any adjective could.
Deliberately left out: pipeline stages, engine and model names, badges, charts, confidence scores, timelines, logos, any second accent color, and every metric that describes the system instead of the customer's week. The tech already had its screens; this one belongs to the buyer.
